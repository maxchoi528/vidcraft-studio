Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-11-18

100% Local-First Design: VidCraft Studio Pro operates strictly inside your browser without analytics trackers, telemetry beacons, or developer-operated cloud databases.

1. Video Metadata & Transcripts

Captions and transcripts extracted from YouTube, Bilibili, TikTok, Douyin, or HTML5 video elements stay in local browser memory and chrome.storage.local strictly on your machine.

2. User-Configured AI & Audio Services

When you supply your own Google Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, or OpenAI API key, your key is stored locally inside browser extension storage and sent directly over HTTPS to that chosen provider only when you click Generate or Audio Whisper.

3. License Activation

Optional Pro license activation sends only the entered Gumroad license key directly to https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify.

4. Data Collection Statement

VidCraft Studio Pro collects, transmits, or sells zero personal data, browsing history, or user transcripts to the developer or third-party ad networks.
