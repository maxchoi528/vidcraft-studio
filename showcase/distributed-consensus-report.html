Executive Summary · TL;DR

Tail-latency amplification degrades synchronous RPC fan-out past 8 shards. Combining hedged requests at the local p95 trigger budget (18ms), cancellation propagation, and strict 5% retry token buckets cuts production p99 from 580ms down to 31ms.

[00:12] Tail Latency Amplification in Microservice Fan-Out

When staging tests run under low load, single-hop tail latency stays below 14ms. Once an aggregator fans out to 20 backend shards concurrently, overall request completion waits for the slowest shard.

KEY POINT

At a 1% per-leaf tail probability across 20 parallel leaf RPCs, end-to-end probability of hitting slow p99 surges from 1% to 18.2%.
